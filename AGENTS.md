## Shared workstation policy

Follow the [shared execution resource protocol](https://github.com/FedorMilovanov/AuditRepo/blob/main/LOCAL_EXECUTION_RESOURCE_PROTOCOL.md) (AuditRepo main). Minimum independent rules:

- QUALITY FIRST, RESOURCE SMART: select the most effective authorized execution surface by total time, tokens, RDC credits, CI queue, local load and evidence fidelity. GitHub/API normally suits simple repository reads/status; hosted CI normally suits full matrices. RDC is welcome for efficient local batches, worktree edits, local file analysis and short verification when it materially improves speed, quality or cost. LIGHT (short reads/commands/edits) and genuinely bounded MEDIUM (incremental build/file transform/isolated short probe) are autonomous, with proportionate MEDIUM risk checks and owned cleanup; no per-call approval, proof of failed alternatives, fixed call cap or heavy slot. Sustained/uncertain pressure, full builds, persistent browsers/servers and bulk media/ASR are HEAVY. Never relabel to evade admission. Batch related work, retain one process/call ID, compact logs, reconcile uncertain outcomes before retrying; no redundant polls/downloads, repeated inference for identical evidence, or account switching to evade limits.
- Heavy Windows tasks require fresh shared admission and Commit/RAM/disk counters: warn at 75%, deny new heavy jobs at 80%, diagnose at 90%; free RAM >=6 GiB, free disk >=15 GiB (or larger artifact reserve). Unknown admission means CI/HOLD. One heavy task workstation-wide before verified controller rollout; after rollout respect its actual enforced slots (initially one; maximum two only after measured acceptance). No parallel engines.
- No local Windows WebKit/CUDA by default. Explicit owner-approved bounded exceptions only; do not change the speech model, quantization, stress dictionary, runtime provider, or fidelity silently. Browser probe 10 min / heavy QA 20 min; extended media tasks need a justified deadline. Close owned children in finally; external watchdog, no blanket termination of owner or unrelated processes.
- Preserve current licensing and provenance, Node test gates, actual browser playback and downstream release evidence as separate checks. Report meaningful phase outcomes, actual RDC calls where useful, evidence, cleanup and remaining HOLD; no per-call ceremony.
